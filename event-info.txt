  LAST_UPDATED: May 2026

  PROHIBITED_BEHAVIORS:
    - Bullying, intimidation, or discrimination
    - Offensive comments regarding gender, sexuality, identity, appearance, race, nationality, religion, or disability
    - Sexual remarks, unwanted flirting, or inappropriate advances
    - Touching others without consent
    - Stalking or blocking someone's movement
    - Photography or recording without permission where privacy is expected
    - Repeated messaging, calling, or contacting after being asked to stop
    - Intentional disruption of lectures, workshops, or programming
    - Mocking or harassing people for being part of furry, therian, cosplay, or alternative communities
    - Harassment related to restroom use or gender identity
    - Threats, stalking, or intimidation
    - Intentional damage to costumes, artwork, merchandise, or personal belongings
  CONSENT:
    - Cosplay, fursuit, or alternative clothing is not consent.
    - If someone tells you your behavior is inappropriate or unwanted, you must stop immediately.
    - If someone asks you to leave them alone, you must respect that.

    - Report harassment to organizers, volunteers, or staff.

    - Reports are taken seriously and handled confidentially.
  CONSEQUENCES:
    - Violations may result in removal from the event without compensation.
    - Serious violations may be reported to authorities.

    - Harassment is determined by how the affected person feels, regardless of the perpetrator's intent.
