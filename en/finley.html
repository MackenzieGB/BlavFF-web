Who is Finley?

Finley is a silver fox and the mascot of our festival. They use they/them pronouns and are here to remind everyone that BlavFF is a space for anyone - no matter who you are or how you identify.

Finley's character came from the idea that the furry community is about being yourself. The fox as a symbol of intelligence, playfulness and adaptability - exactly what we want BlavFF to represent.
