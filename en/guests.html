Who's Attending BlavFF

Guests, presenters and special guests

Presenters

Lucifri

Cosplayer with a love for Hatsune Miku and bbno$. She'll be giving a talk at BlavFF called "Bibinos a.k.a. the King of LARP" about what makes bbno$ so unique.

Visit Instagram →

Diana

Diana is a psychology student focusing her work on the psychosocial aspects of the Furry community. She'll be giving a talk at BlavFF about what connects this subculture and what place it holds in today's society.

Artist Alley

LemonFangz

Well-known Slovak furry artist and fursuit maker with a strong presence in the community. Specializes in fursuit packs and tails, accessories, stickers, prints, keychains, and custom portraits.

Visit Website →

SolverPaw

SolverPaw is a young artist, cosplayer, and animator. She offers custom and premade therian gear of high quality: therian masks, kemonomimi ears, fursuit tails, kandi jewelry, and sticker packs.

Visit TikTok →

Kitty Corner

Kitty Corner is an artist booth focused on cat-themed art. They offer original illustrations, keychains, stickers, prints, bags, and handmade items like crocheted animals and beaded charms.

Visit Behance →

Rocky's Studio

Rocky's Studio is a talented artist and fursuit maker. He specializes in crafting high-quality fursuit paws and original digital and traditional art. At BlavFF, you'll find his work that combines precision with creative design.

Visit WhatsApp →

Korlex

Korlex is a fashion student and versatile crafter from Bratislava. They work with up-cycled clothing, jewellery, accessories, fursuits, and denim creations. Their goal is to spread positivity and encourage others to create despite perfectionism.

Visit Instagram →

Vixenna

Vixenna is an artist working in both digital and traditional media. She offers stickers, pendants, art prints with animal and furry themes, and custom waiting badges. Although she mainly draws people, her portfolio also features plenty of animal motifs.

Visit Instagram →
