By using our services, you agree to the processing of data within the scope specified below.

2. What data we process

We may process in particular:

  • •name and nickname,
  • •email address,
  • •phone number,
  • •IP address,
  • •technical data about device and browser,
  • •event registration data,
  • •analytical data about website usage,
  • •communication data sent via forms or chat.

3. Purposes of processing

We process personal data for the purpose of:

  • •organizing events,
  • •participant registration,
  • •communication with visitors,
  • •ensuring website functionality,
  • •traffic analysis,
  • •improving services and security,
  • •handling user requests.

4. Third-party services used

We use third-party services on our websites that may process data according to their own terms.

4.1 Google Tag Manager

We use Google Tag Manager to manage analytics and marketing tools. GTM itself does not collect personal data, but enables loading of additional services (Google Analytics, Google Ads).

More: Google Privacy Policy

4.2 Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to measure traffic and website analytics. The service uses cookies to identify unique visitors and track on-site behaviour.

Google may collect: IP address, device data, information about website usage.

More: Google Privacy Policy

4.3 Google Ads

We use Google Ads to measure conversions from advertising campaigns. The service stores cookies when a user clicks on our ad.

More: Google Privacy Policy

4.4 Microsoft Clarity

We use Microsoft Clarity for website usage analysis, heatmaps and anonymized tracking of user interactions.

The service may collect: mouse movement, clicks, scrolling, technical data about the device.

More: Microsoft Privacy Statement

4.5 New Relic

We use New Relic Browser Monitoring to monitor website performance, track errors and ensure site reliability.

The service collects: page load performance data, JavaScript errors, interaction metrics, session replay (session recording). Data is stored in session storage, not in cookies.

More: New Relic Privacy Policy

4.6 tawk.to

We use tawk.to to provide live chat and customer support.

When using chat, the following may be processed: message content, IP address, technical data about the device.

More: tawk.to Privacy Policy

4.7 Pretix

We use the Pretix system for participant registration and ticket management.

Pretix may process: name, email, registration data, payment information.

More: Pretix Privacy Information

4.8 Google Maps

Our pages may use embedded Google Maps.

Google may process when loading the map: IP address, device data, cookies.

More: Google Privacy Policy
